About FurniLab

Designed. Developed. Manufactured by us.

FurniLab is an independent outdoor furniture manufacturer creating distinctive furniture for residential, hospitality and commercial spaces. From concept and material selection through to production and finishing, we manufacture our furniture ourselves, with no third-party manufacturers or intermediaries.

Our 2026 collection brings together timber, metal, woven wicker, polycement and sintered stone, creating furniture that combines contemporary design, functionality and craftsmanship.

Built to last outdoors

Every steel frame gets two protective layers, the same ED coating technology used on cars, then a powder coating on top. The result is a rust-resistant, premium finish.

  1. Step 1 · CleanA clean startThe coating bonds firmly to the steel.
  2. Step 2 · ED coatingRust protection inside and outReaches every corner and inside the tubes.
  3. Step 3 · Powder coatingLasting colourA tough, even coloured layer for everyday wear.
  4. Step 4 · Bake and cureA smooth, even finishBaked hard for everyday outdoor use.
